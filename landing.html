🎯 Gameplay

Fight through waves of ghosts using fireballs, lightning, and shield spells. Each wave gets progressively harder, introducing tank ghosts, shooter ghosts, and powerful bosses.

Collect health potions, mana mushrooms, and upgrade pills to enhance your wizard's abilities!

1

2
